Storm chaser

Finds where thunderstorms are most likely in the next 12 hours, then routes you there in time.

    ⚠️ This is forecast guidance, not a warning system. Never drive into a hail core, rain-wrapped area or flooded road. Always keep an escape route and follow official NWS warnings.

    Storm watcher

    Finds a safe, dry spot with a clear view of a storm: cloud structure by day, lightning after dark.

    Finds the best spot on the coast to watch big surf and wind, and the best time you can get there (next 36 hours).

      ⚡ Lightning can strike 10+ miles from a storm, out of clear sky. Watch from inside a hard-top car, not standing beside it. If you hear thunder, you're in range. Don't park under trees, on hilltops in the open, or in dips that can flood, and keep an escape route open behind you.

      🌊 Storm waves run far further up the beach than you expect, and "sneaker waves" come without warning. Watch from high ground or your car, never turn your back on the ocean, and stay off jetties, rocks and driftwood logs (waves roll them). In high wind, avoid parking under trees.
